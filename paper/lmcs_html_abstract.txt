This paper studies public coherence obligations created when a fixed cubical
sealed-extension calculus is enlarged by globally acting structural layers such
as modalities, universe formers, or promoted interface packages.  The main
result is a depth-two theorem for this fixed calculus: unary trace is
insufficient, because univalence creates genuine binary naturality obligations,
but structural trace generated above binary arity is not primitive.  Higher
structural obligations are represented by cubical open-box extension data
computed from lower-arity boundaries, so realized obligation objects stabilize
at binary trace and normalized minimal public signatures contain no primitive
structural trace field above binary arity.  A separate counting result gives a
two-step recurrence only under explicit full-coupling and constant-payload
hypotheses.  The accompanying mechanization checks the theorem-facing
fixed-calculus components and representative adequacy bridges; transfer to
other cubical foundations remains conditional on the stated adequacy interface.
